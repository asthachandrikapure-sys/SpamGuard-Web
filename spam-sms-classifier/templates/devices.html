Setup Guide

How to Connect SpamGuard Android

1

Open Android Studio

Open the project at spam-sms-classifier/android/ in Android Studio.

2

Configure Server URL

In app/build.gradle.kts, set API_BASE_URL to your Flask backend URL (e.g., http://192.168.x.x:5000/ for local network).

3

Build & Install on Phone

Connect your Android phone via USB, run the app. Grant SMS permission and Notification permission.

4

Enable Auto Protection

In the SpamGuard app, toggle Auto Protection to ON. The device will appear as CONNECTED above within 20 seconds.

5

SMS Auto-Analysis Active

Every incoming SMS will now be automatically classified by SpamGuard AI. High-risk spam triggers an on-device notification.

🔒 Privacy Guarantee

SpamGuard never reads your existing SMS inbox, never forwards messages, never deletes messages, and never sends SMS automatically. Only newly received messages are analyzed when you enable protection.
