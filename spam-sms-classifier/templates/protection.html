Setup

Connecting Your Android Device

1

Install SpamGuard Android App

Build and install the companion app from spam-sms-classifier/android/ using Android Studio.

2

Grant SMS Permission

When prompted, grant the RECEIVE_SMS permission so SpamGuard can monitor incoming messages.

3

Enable Auto Protection

In the SpamGuard app, toggle Auto Protection ON to begin sending incoming SMS to the AI backend.

4

Watch the Dashboard Update

This page refreshes every 15 seconds. When your phone sends its first heartbeat, status will show CONNECTED.
