About the Project

An engineering project demonstrating the application of NLP and ML for SMS classification.

🚫

What is Spam SMS?

Spam SMS messages are unsolicited, bulk text messages typically sent to promote commercial offers, conduct phishing attacks, or attempt fraud. They often contain suspicious links, prize offers, or urgent requests for personal information.

✅

What is Ham SMS?

Ham SMS messages are legitimate, wanted messages from known contacts or trusted services. These include personal conversations, genuine notifications, appointment reminders, and other non-promotional communications.

⚙

How Does the System Work?

The system applies Natural Language Processing to preprocess text, extracts TF-IDF features, and passes them to a trained Machine Learning classifier. The result is stored in a database for further analysis.
